# Project Description - NobleGoldFish
NobleGoldFish Skateshop application. For the 2025 business year, NobleGoldFish recorded approximately $3 million in sales with an average sale amounting to $300. At nearly 10,000 orders processed, 7,000 customers served, and a growth rate of 10%, NobleGoldFish Skateshop requires a database management system that supports data organization and scalability for their business growth.
The requirements of NobleGoldFish’s new database management system are the following:   

    Efficiently manage customer information   
    Track orders and order status   
    Organize manufacturer and product information.    
    Maintain data integrity between the Customer, Orders, Products, and Manufacturer tables by using primary and foreign keys.
    Utilize the table OrderProducts to maintain the M:N relationshiop for order's product details.
